The Woman Behind the Treasure

Caroline Treasure is a multi-talented Nigerian entrepreneur whose hands never rest. From the sewing machine to the mixing bowl, from the perfume still to the skincare jar — everything she touches becomes a piece of her story.

She sews for both men and women. She bakes every kind of pastry. She handcrafts cosmetics for adults and infants, blends Arabic and designer perfumes, designs bags and t-shirts, and serves up fresh parfaits, smoothies, chapman and herbal zobo.

And there's more — she sells premium fabrics, produces car wash and hair gel, and is constantly creating new products. If it can be made with love, Caroline has probably already made it.
